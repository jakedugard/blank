**[Download blank.dmg](https://github.com/jakedugard/blank/releases/latest/download/blank.dmg)** for Mac. Open it, drag blank to Applications. It updates itself from here on.

The other files are for the updater and can be ignored.

## What's new

- **Zap.** Live sites come with a cookie banner, a chat bubble, a badge, and
  you can't edit the site. Press Zap in the bar, click the thing, and it's
  gone from every take of that site. It hides in place, so nothing around it
  moves; ⌥-click removes it from the layout instead. ⌘Z takes one back,
  right-click the pill to restore any by name.
- **Output Size.** ••• → Recording now offers 1× alongside the 2× default:
  the page at CSS size, half the dimensions, for the web or a DM.
- **Show Cursor.** Leave the pointer in a take for a click-through. Off by
  default, and the hide now holds on sites that keep their cursors inside
  web components.
- **Smoother recordings.** The recorder now keeps one frame per frame macOS
  captures instead of sampling on a timer that ran slightly fast; about one
  frame in nine of a scroll used to be a repeat. A take of a still page
  records its full length, and the settle at the end of a scroll is kept.
