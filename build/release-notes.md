**[Download blank.dmg](https://github.com/jakedugard/blank/releases/latest/download/blank.dmg)** for Mac. Open it, drag blank to Applications. It updates itself from here on.

The other files are for the updater and can be ignored.

## What's new

- **Fixed: a take that recorded nothing.** Recording could fail silently and leave Record stuck on until you quit blank. It was intermittent, and it has been there since recording shipped — the encoder waited on something that never came back, while blank went on believing a take was running.
- The menu bar icon carries a quiet dot on its corner while an update is waiting, and the menu says **Downloading…** with a percentage while it comes down. Pressing Check for Updates… used to look like nothing had happened, because finding an update was the one case it said nothing about.
- The recording indicator is now a red dot in the middle of the icon. Both indicators are visible whatever your menu bar looks like; the old ones could vanish into a dark menu bar on a Mac set to light appearance.
