**[Download blank.dmg](https://github.com/jakedugard/blank/releases/latest/download/blank.dmg)** for Mac. Open it, drag blank to Applications. It updates itself from here on.

The other files are for the updater and can be ignored.

## What's new

- **Fixed: the first take asked for permission and then took the question back.** If blank doesn't have Screen Recording permission yet, the Record button now reads **Allow recording ↗** and stays there until you've granted it, instead of flashing past in four seconds like an ordinary error. Grant it, come back to blank, and the button returns to Record on its own.
